About Our School

Learning that builds character, not just grades

Founded in 2001, Al-Noor Public School combines a strong academic curriculum with modern teaching methods, digital classrooms and a culture of respect and responsibility.

  • 1Qualified faculty — experienced teachers with regular training and mentoring.
  • 2Smart classrooms — projectors, computer lab and science labs for practical learning.
  • 3Small class sizes — maximum 25 students so every child gets attention.
  • 4Co-curricular activities — sports, debates, art, coding club and annual events.
Meet Our Faculty

Our Mission

To provide affordable, high-quality education that prepares students for the challenges of the modern world while keeping strong moral values at the centre.

  • Student-first approach in every decision
  • Safe, inclusive and disciplined environment
  • Focus on critical thinking and creativity
  • Strong partnership with parents
  • Continuous improvement through feedback
